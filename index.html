METHODOLOGY • ZERO SHORTCUTS

The 5-Stage Studio Protocol

Every vehicle undergoes our systematic, surgical multi-step restoration before leaving our studio bay.

01
STAGE 01 • DIAGNOSTICS

Comprehensive Inspection

We inspect the vehicle under high-CRI detailing inspection lighting and record paint thickness across all panels using ultrasonic digital depth gauges to identify thin spots and factory clear coat levels.

  • Digital paint depth mapping (Microns)
  • Scratch & rock-chip defect assessment sheet
  • Consultation with car owner on goals
02
STAGE 02 • DECONTAMINATION

Preparation & Chemical Bath

Deep cleaning and multi-stage surface decontamination. pH-neutral thick snow foam loosens grime, iron remover dissolves embedded metallic brake dust, and synthetic clay removes stubborn industrial fallout.

  • pH-Neutral snow foam soak & 3-bucket wash
  • Chemical iron fallout & tar removal
  • Fine synthetic clay bar surface smoothing
03
STAGE 03 • RESTORATION

Precision Paint Correction

Paint imperfections, swirl marks, oxidation, and scratches are surgically corrected where required. Dual-action machine polishers with graded pads restore optical clarity and deep gloss without thinning your clear coat.

  • Panel taping & sensitive trim masking
  • Heavy compounding for scratch & swirl removal
  • Finishing polish to achieve museum mirror gloss
04
STAGE 04 • BARRIER

Protection & Film Application

PPF, ceramic coating, or selected treatment is professionally applied. PPF is precision-installed in our positive-pressure clean bay; ceramic coatings are leveled and cross-linked panel by panel.

  • IPA panel wipe down for pure bonding
  • Optical TPU film installation with wrapped edges
  • Multi-layer ceramic or graphene application
05
STAGE 05 • QUALITY ASSURANCE

Final Inspection & Handover

Every millimeter is checked before delivery under multi-spectrum lighting. Coatings receive short-wave infrared heat lamp curing. We review aftercare maintenance guidelines with you upon vehicle handover.

  • Short-wave infrared (IR) heat lamp cure
  • 50-Point studio quality handover checklist
  • Client handover & aftercare kit orientation
